## 3. Value chain section

**Section heading**
How the NORMIA flow works

**Intro**
A construction project is only as reliable as the information that follows the work. NORMIA turns execution into a connected chain from task to delivery.

**Value chain steps**
1. Plan task – Define work and control points before the crew starts.
2. Execute work – Complete tasks in the field with clear guidance.
3. Capture photo – Record evidence where the work happens.
4. Perform control – Verify results and close deviations.
5. Generate documentation – Turn field input into delivery-ready records.
6. Deliver FDV – Hand over the right documentation with the finished project.

**Closing note**
Each step is linked so nothing is lost between the site, the office and the delivery package.
